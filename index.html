01 / ABOUT

PHYSICAL SCIENCE × COMPUTATION

From atmospheric physics to actionable prediction.

I am a PhD scholar in Atmospheric and Ocean Sciences at NIT Rourkela. My research focuses on developing location-specific forecasting systems for monsoon-induced extremes by integrating WRF-ARW numerical modelling with machine learning.

I work with large-scale satellite, reanalysis and model datasets, including ERA5, IMERG, IMDAA and WRF outputs. My interests span extreme rainfall, monsoon dynamics, ENSO/IOD teleconnections, tropical cyclones and climate risk.

01PhD · 2025—Present

Location-specific forecasting of monsoon-induced extremes

My doctoral research aims to develop a location-specific forecasting system by integrating high-resolution numerical weather prediction with machine learning. The work focuses on understanding the physical and dynamical characteristics of monsoon-induced extremes, quantifying model errors, and improving forecast skill through data-driven approaches.

The broader workflow links WRF-ARW simulations, observational and reanalysis datasets, extreme-event diagnostics and ML/DL-based error characterization to move from physical understanding toward more reliable local forecasts.
